about[en]=GDB-Attacher is a 7-step wizard to attach photos and documents to Esri FileGDB features.<br><br>Pick the source layer (only FileGDB layers already loaded in the project), the plugin checks that the geodatabase is writable through GDAL OpenFileGDB <b>and that the attachment table exists with its 6 fields</b>: if it is missing or incomplete the wizard stops and points you to ArcGIS Pro (EnableAttachments). The plugin never creates the attachment table.<br><br>Then field discovery suggests the photo fields, you choose the base folder and the fields, you decide the attachment name (original name, per-feature QGIS formula, or a file list from CSV joined on GLOBALID), you review the preview with counts, the batch runs in a single transaction (optional, bypassable backup) and finally the attachment table style with photo previews is applied.<br><br>Italian/English interface. GPLv2+ license.
